# Island Spice website concept

A single-page storefront and staff dashboard for Island Spice, built on the Flying Goat layout.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static host (GitHub Pages works as is).

## What's in it

- **Storefront:** a heat index of 11 sauces and seasonings ordered from mild to fiery, a spice compass that matches products to taste, a build-a-box gift builder, the salt & sugar free line, office contacts and a stockist enquiry form.
- **Admin (`#admin`):** inventory by location (Kingston, Miami, Montreal, Amazon FBA), Amazon listings and FBA replenishment, the California retail pipeline, wholesale accounts and a roadmap.

## Notes

- Product names, sizes, descriptions and ingredients come from the 2025 Island Spice catalog.
- Heat ratings (1–5) are estimates from the catalog descriptions and need confirming.
- Every number on the admin page is sample data. Checkout, forms and admin actions show a confirmation only; nothing is sent or saved to a server.
- Product images are cut out of the catalog pages and live in `images/`.
